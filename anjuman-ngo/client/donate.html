Questions

Donation FAQ

Can I donate through UPI?

Yes. The verified organization UPI QR and UPI account details will be added before the donation system goes live.

Will Razorpay payments be verified?

Yes. Payment confirmation will be verified on the backend before a donation is marked as successful.

Will I receive a donation record?

The backend will maintain transaction records. Receipt functionality can be added once the organization's final receipt and compliance requirements are confirmed.
