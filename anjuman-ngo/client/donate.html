Questions

Donation FAQ

Can I donate without creating an account?

Yes. Donations can be made without signing in. Donor contact information is optional.

How are direct UPI donations confirmed?

After paying through UPI, you submit the transaction reference. The donation remains pending until the payment is verified.

Are Razorpay payments verified?

Yes. A Razorpay Checkout response is not treated as final proof of payment. The server verifies the payment before the donation is marked as verified.

Will I receive a receipt?

Donation records are maintained by the system. Formal receipt functionality will follow the organization's approved receipt and compliance process.
