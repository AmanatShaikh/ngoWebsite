Assistance Services

Find the support that fits your situation

Select one of the four assistance categories below to understand the purpose of the service and begin an application.

Healthcare

Medical Assistance

Medical assistance is intended for applicants seeking support related to genuine healthcare needs.

  • Medical treatment-related requests
  • Supporting medical documents can be submitted with the application
  • Applications can be reviewed and tracked through the user dashboard
Apply for Medical Assistance
Education

Scholarship & Education Support

Education assistance helps eligible students submit requests related to academic support.

  • Student and institution information
  • Academic and fee-related supporting documents
  • Structured review through the application system
Apply for Education Support
Livelihood

Livelihood & Work Support

Livelihood support is designed for eligible requests related to employment, work and economic self-reliance.

  • Employment or livelihood-related needs
  • Applicant background and current situation
  • Supporting records where required
Apply for Livelihood Support
Travel

Essential Travel Assistance

Travel assistance provides a way to submit eligible requests where necessary travel has become difficult to arrange.

  • Origin and destination details
  • Reason and urgency of travel
  • Supporting information where applicable
Apply for Travel Assistance
